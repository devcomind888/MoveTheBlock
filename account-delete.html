Account Deletion

Users can request the deletion of their MoveTheBlock account and associated game data.

To request account deletion, please contact the MoveTheBlock support team with the Google account used to sign in to the game and your in-game UID.

Account Deletion Information

Account deletion may permanently remove your game account and associated game data.

Some information may be retained when required by applicable laws or for legitimate business purposes.
