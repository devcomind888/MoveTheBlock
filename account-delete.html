Account Deletion

Users can request the deletion of their MoveTheBlock account and associated game data.

To request account deletion, please delete the MoveTheBlock account information associated with your Google account. The deletion of the Google account information will initiate the deletion of the associated MoveTheBlock account and game data.

Account Deletion Information

When the account deletion request is processed, your MoveTheBlock account and associated game data may be permanently deleted.

Data That May Be Deleted

  • MoveTheBlock game account
  • In-game UID and account information
  • Game progress and associated game data
  • Google account information associated with the MoveTheBlock account

Data Retention

Some information may be retained when required by applicable laws, regulations, security requirements, fraud prevention, or other legitimate business purposes.

Any retained information will be kept only for the period necessary for the applicable purpose.

Important Information

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Deleted game progress and associated data cannot be recovered.
